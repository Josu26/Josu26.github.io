03 / How I think

Reliability is a product decision.

The interesting work lives in constraints, trade-offs and what happens when the inputs are incomplete.

Make uncertainty visible.

Do not turn missing evidence into a polished, unsupported answer.

Separate intent from implementation.

Document what is shipped, what is tested, and what is still a hypothesis.

Build boundaries into the design.

Access, privacy, provenance and reviewability are part of the product, not a final checklist.

Optimize for real decisions.

A dashboard or automated workflow only matters when it improves a meaningful decision or process.
